About Me

From Curiosity to Production AI Systems

My journey into Artificial Intelligence started with a simple curiosity about how machines learn. That curiosity evolved into a commitment to designing production-ready AI systems, scalable backend services, and intelligent applications capable of solving real-world problems.

I'm Zulkarnain, a Computer Science undergraduate specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Backend Engineering. My work combines software engineering principles with modern AI techniques to build reliable, scalable, and maintainable systems.

During my Artificial Intelligence Internship at Koshur Scientist, I contributed to adaptive learning systems, recommendation engines, learning analytics, and backend APIs using FastAPI. Working on real production workflows taught me that successful AI products require much more than accurate models—they require clean architecture, efficient data pipelines, robust APIs, and excellent engineering practices.

Beyond internships, I continuously build personal projects exploring recommendation systems, computer vision, deep learning, natural language processing, and modern backend development while documenting my learning through technical blogs.
